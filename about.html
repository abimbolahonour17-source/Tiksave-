About TikSave

A simple tool for saving videos responsibly.

TikSave is a lightweight website designed to make it easier to process a TikTok video link and save a video when a download is available. The goal is a clear, straightforward experience without requiring visitors to create an account.

How it works

  1. Copy the link to a TikTok video.
  2. Paste the link into the TikSave downloader and request a preview.
  3. If a supported video is returned, use the download button to save it to your device.

Respect creators

TikSave is intended for videos you created yourself, content you have permission to download, or content you are otherwise legally entitled to save. A publicly accessible link does not automatically mean you have permission to copy, reuse, repost, or commercially exploit the video.
