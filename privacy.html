Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 25, 2026

This policy explains how TikSave handles information when you use this website.

Information you provide

When you submit a TikTok link, the link is sent to TikSave's server so the service can request video information from its third-party processing provider. Do not submit private or sensitive links. The service does not ask you to create an account or provide a name, phone number, or payment details.

Browser preferences

TikSave stores your light or dark theme choice in your browser's local storage. This preference stays on your device and is used to remember your display choice.

Service providers and technical data

TikSave is hosted using Vercel. Video link processing currently uses the third-party TikWM service, and video thumbnails or media may be delivered from third-party servers. Those providers may process technical information such as IP address, request details, and browser information under their own policies. TikSave does not control the privacy practices of those providers.

Cookies and advertising

TikSave does not currently operate a user account system. If advertising services such as Google AdSense are added in the future, those providers may use cookies or similar technologies to serve and measure ads, subject to their own policies and applicable consent requirements. This policy will be updated before advertising is enabled.

Retention and security

Submitted links are used to process the requested video. The website does not provide a user library or saved-download history. Hosting and service providers may retain technical logs according to their own retention practices. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Children

TikSave is not designed to collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the service, please email abimbolahonour2@gmail.com to raise the concern.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the service changes. The effective date above will be revised when material changes are made.

Privacy questions
